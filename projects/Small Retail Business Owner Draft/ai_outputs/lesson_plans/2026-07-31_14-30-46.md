# Lesson Plan

## Qualification
Small Retail Business Owner Draft

## Module
KM-01 - Foundations of Retail Management

## Duration
3.0 hours

## Learning Outcomes

* Identify key principles for successful retail management
* Understand different classifications and types of businesses in the retail industry
* Develop critical thinking skills to analyze business fundamentals and make informed decisions
* Apply knowledge to create effective business plans and strategies

## Learning Objectives

* Upon completing this module, learners will be able to:
	+ Explain the importance of market research and analysis in retail management
	+ Classify different types of retail businesses (e.g., brick-and-mortar, e-commerce, subscription-based)
	+ Describe key principles for creating effective marketing strategies and promotional campaigns
	+ Develop a basic business plan outline, including financial projections and operational strategies

## Introduction

Welcome to KM-01 - Foundations of Retail Management! This module is designed to provide a comprehensive introduction to the retail industry, focusing on business fundamentals, classifications, and key principles for success. Through interactive lessons and activities, learners will develop critical thinking skills and apply knowledge to create effective business plans and strategies.

## Lesson Activities

1. **Retail Industry Overview**: Explore the history, trends, and current state of the retail industry.
2. **Classification of Retail Businesses**: Learn about different types of retail businesses, including brick-and-mortar, e-commerce, and subscription-based models.
3. **Key Principles for Successful Retail Management**: Discuss essential principles for creating effective marketing strategies, promotional campaigns, and business plans.
4. **Business Plan Development**: Work in groups to develop a basic business plan outline, including financial projections and operational strategies.

## Facilitator Notes

* Emphasize the importance of market research and analysis throughout the lesson.
* Encourage learners to ask questions and share their experiences with retail management.
* Circulate around the room to provide individualized support and guidance.

## Learner Activities

1. **Retail Industry Research Project**: Complete a brief research project on a specific retail industry trend or challenge.
2. **Case Study Analysis**: Analyze a real-life case study of a successful retail business, identifying key principles for success.
3. **Group Discussion Forum**: Participate in an online discussion forum to share ideas and best practices with peers.

## Formative Assessment

* Quizzes throughout the lesson to assess learners' understanding of key concepts.
* Group presentations on business plan development.

## Reflection

* Take time to reflect on what was learned during the lesson.
* Ask questions or provide feedback on how the facilitator can improve future lessons.

## Homework

* Complete a self-assessment quiz on key concepts covered in the lesson.
* Develop a basic marketing strategy for a hypothetical retail business.
