# Data Governance and Integrity - Curriculum Development Workshop

---

## Module Information

*   **Course Title:** Data Governance and Integrity – Understanding Principles & Practising Best Practices
*   **Duration:** 2 hours, approximately.
*   **Target Audience:** Individuals new to data governance, focusing on foundational concepts and practical application of best practices.  (Beginner level). Technical proficiency is not required; a focus on understanding principles-driven strategies is essential.
 *   **Learning Goals:** Upon completion of this workshop, participants will be able to clearly define data quality; identify potential data issues; demonstrate understanding of key governance tools; and apply basic integrity measures in a simulated scenario. 

## Facilitator Opening (15 mins)

“Welcome everyone to the Data Governance and Integrity – Fundamentals Workshop. My name is [Facilitation Name], and I’ll be facilitating this course today. The goal of this workshop isn’t just about understanding concepts; it's about applying them effectively in your daily workflow, safeguarding data trustworthiness, ensuring compliance—and ultimately improving decision-making. We're going to delve into how organizations establish a bedrock of integrity within their datasets, reducing risks and bolstering insights. Getting comfortable with the principles first is incredibly helpful later when you've absorbed key concepts."

“We cover basic definitions, issues impacting reliability, the role of governance teams, different tool capabilities (governance platforms), but most importantly – we want to understand how each one operates; so you get hands-on experience with practice exercises.” 


## Icebreaker - Collaborative Brainstorming (5 mins) -* *

“Let’s briefly go around and do an quick activity. I’m going to ask some basic questions about your experiences with data handling.  There are no right or wrong answers – we're exploring a common reality here." 


To start, can each of you identify the first data concept that comes to mind when you hear the term ‘Data Governance’? Let's take three moments each; and we will share out together in groups—there should be minimal holding.”


## Introduction (10 mins) - Setting the Stage

“Alright! Excellent.  Let’s start with an introductory overview. The core focus here isn't on advanced methodologies or complex tools – it's focused on fundamental aspects which are widely applicable and critical to how organizations manage data assets.” 

“Think of data governance as establishing a 'safety net' around your data—it doesn’t guarantee perfect results, but establishes an expectation you should be trustworthy in the context that these inputs get used - ensuring its quality remains and this is backed up with policies. It will lead to consistent values throughout the entire lifecycle and reduce risks caused by low-quality or duplicated data."

"Understanding data governance sets the foundation for reliable insights from these raw inputs.”



## Section 1: What is Data Quality? – Defining Key Terminology (20 mins) -

“Let’s begin with understanding ‘data quality.’  It isn’t just about being ‘right’; it’s about how well your data *meets the needs* of your processes and users. A concept used to provide you a foundation for this subject is, what we call ‘acceptable risk.’ This can be very important when considering your own workflow from now on!”

“Let's break down several facets: Accuracy – does it reflect what happened?  Completeness– is the data available? Consistency - Across different sources of the same data are they the same and not contradictory? Timeliness - Is the information accessible when users need this time-efficiently?" 



*   **Activity Prompt:** "Pause for a moment.” Ask learners to silently jot down one concrete example illustrating an accuracy issue in their own work—a mistake made while inputting data."

  “After about fifteen minutes, have each person share with the group what they jot down -- this will give you additional insight into your prior understanding!” (Pause for 3-4 exchanges)


## Section 2: Types of Data Governance – Roles and Responsibilities (20 mins) - *** *

"Now, let’s explore common data governance roles. These roles, and the tasks are very different across organizations; but they broadly fall under categories like ‘data owners,’ ‘compliance officers’, 'Quality Champions,' as well as other areas such as ‘Metadata managers,' that drive this framework”

“Let’s go through a brief discussion of each—what can be expect and where does the work reside.” “What should companies consider to do with these teams to ensure proper oversight?” 



* **Quick Check:** ‘Can you identify three stakeholders within your own organization who directly or indirectly impact data quality?' (Brief, targeted list).
*   **Discussion Prompt**:  'Imagine a new scenario. A reporting system consistently provides inaccurate figures; where would the priority be in addressing this and why?' (Provide discussion options): “Consider compliance regulations as a prioritization.”




## Practical Activity: Data Issue Identification – Case Study Challenge (20 minutes) -

“Let’s dive into a mini-workshop. I'm going to give you a simple case study – this isn’t graded; it's really for understanding ‘how does data quality matter’." 


*   **(Scenario presented as bullet points):** “A customer database contains address information that has inconsistent format in multiple countries and is known to have an error rate of 10% – this causes issues with sales targets. We would run it from each location individually -- where would this likely lead to?”
*   “As a role, *you must*** - identify at least three potential data quality issues presented” (Pause) "Think through the process and provide brief explanations for why.”

    *   **Quick feedback questions:** “Does your analysis consider legal requirements as factors for prioritization?"


## Common Questions & Suggested Answers (5 mins)

“Now, some common questions and solutions. Here are a few…  Let's discuss some potential challenges: *Q: "How does establishing data quality standards directly impact the cost of projects?"* **A:** “Improved efficiency is a key driver because this prevents rework caused by errors - you reduce time spent trying to fix that.”    * Q: “Can we make it about business requirements?” – A : *It's about adding data products to your dashboard, improving reporting accuracy*;  **Q: "When do you start doing these things?*" - A:** “It begins with understanding what business objectives are crucial to maintaining trust in the application of this analysis. Think data that should not be lost!”.”



## Lesson Summary (5 mins) – Summarizing Core Concepts


“Essentially, data quality starts with identifying where high-risk issues could lead your insights in ways we can use”  This is fundamental – data accuracy, consistency and timeliness all build up a secure foundation when using these insights. These are key challenges of any organization when deploying these data strategies.”


## Homework Instructions (10 mins) - – Assessment**

“For this homework, I want you to choose *ONE* significant data quality issue impacting your department’s operational tasks—a mistake in the dataset causing operational problems. Think realistically which part could require investment and prioritize.” Then, you must draft a one-sentence explanation of why that particular issue is important – with potential mitigation strategies." 


“Do this individually -  I want to review the solutions/strategies; so make sure your thinking aligns directly to these criteria.”


## Closing Remarks (3 mins)

"Before we wrap up; remember data governance isn’t a one-size-fits-all approach, but consistent effort will build trust and reliability for every dataset throughout." “I strongly encourage you to keep questioning about the systems they operate in – and how good their understanding of these core concepts are!” - Thank You! We are so glad this has served your first part of our workshop. Do you have any questions before I go?”  (Open a brief Q&A period).
