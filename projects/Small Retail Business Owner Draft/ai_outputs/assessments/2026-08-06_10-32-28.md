# Knowledge Assessment – KT-01-B (Retail Industry)

**Module:** KT-01-B
**Difficulty:** Moderate
**Number of Questions**: 10
**Requirements**: Section A: 10 multiple-choice questions, four options each, one correct answer.  Section B: 10 short-answer questions. Section C: 5 scenario-based questions. Marked marking guide provided below.

---

### **Section A - Multiple Choice Questions (4 marks each)** – Please select the BEST answer for each question

1. Which of the following is a fundamental pillar of retail success?
    a) Trendy clothing selection only.
    b) Providing consistently quality products at competitive prices.
    c) Leveraging advanced technology exclusively.
    d) Exclusive distribution channels only.

2.  Strategic planning in retail is MOST focused on:
        a) Increasing market share rapidly
 
         b) Creating consistent value through operational efficiency
 
         c) Prioritizing revenue over customer satisfaction
		d) Maintaining a rigid brand image

3.  What does the 'customer journey' encompass as a part of retail?
    a) Simply focusing on product display
	b) Defining the entire process customers undergo before and after a purchase 
     c) Running marketing campaigns without considering customer interactions
    d) Creating sales promotions with little thought for user behavior

4. What does the 'Net Promoter Score' (NPS) measure?
		a) A store’s overall image through advertising
	b) Customer loyalty and relationship management potential, based on their likelihood to recommend 
		c) The items stocked in a retailer's stores
		d) Retail outlet location map display

5.  Why is effective data analytics crucial for retail decision-making? 
	a) For making quick decisions during the day.
 
	b) Allows retailers to understand and react quickly to consumer needs, market trends, and operational efficiencies 	
	c) Primarily for ensuring product shelf presentation 
		d) Simply getting more revenue from customers 

### **Section B - Short Answer Questions (5 marks each)** – Please answer the questions in full sentences.  Answer concisely and demonstrating a clear understanding of the concept.

6. Explain the difference between loyalty programs and discount schemes within a retail environment. Give a specific example for each .

7. Briefly, explain the role of market research in developing an effective retailer’s strategic plan. Provide a scenario where this plan may be important. 


8.  How would you describe the importance of understanding customer segmentation when optimizing marketing? Illustrate your response with just two practical examples using retail sector examples.

9. Define and list **three** specific strategies that retailers can implement to retain loyal customers. Your answer should only utilize single sentence form, based on those of a retail consultant experience .


10. Differentiate between 'brand awareness' and brand perception in relation to retail businesses. Give the key differences you note from a point of view for an experienced retailer

### **Section C - Scenario-Based Questions (5 marks each)** – Respond as if you are formulating this business plan , taking account that a retailer is experiencing decline .


11. A new chain has just launched in a local town and received less success than expected compared to its competitors.  Provide at least three potential reasons for this phenomenon and suggest one actionable step the retail operator can take .

 
12. A significant number of online retailers are offering low-cost products but focusing on very minimal supply chains. Analyze how these practices could negatively impact the value of businesses based in a particular sector within your setting . Include two specific strategies or interventions that would be necessary to mitigate this effect . 


13. Discuss two ways the introduction into the retail industry of sustainable and ethically-sourced resources presents challenges for small retail outlets , explain at least one mitigation strategy for each challenge addressed above

14. Retailers are currently struggling to generate a sufficient return on investment (ROI). Suggest at least three practical strategies from knowledge learned in past studies that an experienced retail operator could use to increase profit margins and ROI?
